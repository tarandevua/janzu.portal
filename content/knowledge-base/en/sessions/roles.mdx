## Administrator

An **Administrator** manages the platform, community records, and certification operations.

### Responsibilities

- Invite, approve, deactivate, and manage users.
- Assign and remove portal roles.
- Verify existing community members and historical training records.
- Remove a profile from directories when administrative safety support is required. Only the member can opt their profile into a directory.
- Review certification eligibility and assessment results.
- Approve certification and activate Facilitator status.
- Issue, correct, or revoke digital certificates.
- Manage events, locations, agreements, and platform settings.
- Resolve account, access, and data problems.
- Review audit records for important platform actions.

### Access

Administrators have full access to the portal. Sensitive actions, including role changes, certification decisions, and certificate changes, should always be recorded in an audit log.

## Instructor

An **Instructor** teaches Janzu and guides Trainees through training, supervised practice, and certification.

### Responsibilities

- Deliver or record Level 1 and Level 2 training.
- Accept and supervise assigned Trainees.
- Review Trainee practice and certification progress.
- Provide guidance and feedback during the learning process.
- Confirm when a Trainee is ready to continue to the next level.
- Reinforce and explain the Learning Alliance and certification process.
- Guide Trainees toward their final assessment.
- Participate in assessments when authorized.

### Access

Instructors can access the training and certification information of their assigned Trainees. They should not automatically receive full administrative access or access to unrelated community members.

## Trainee

A **Trainee** is learning Janzu and progressing through training, supervised practice, and certification.

### Responsibilities

- Accept the Learning Alliance or Learning Contract.
- Complete their personal and training profile.
- Select or request an Instructor.
- Record their Level 1 and Level 2 training history.
- Register practice sessions.
- Request and receive feedback from Session Participants.
- Track validated sessions and certification milestones.
- Follow guidance provided by their Instructor.
- Request the next training or assessment step after meeting its requirements.
- Keep their contact, location, and profile visibility preferences up to date.

### Access

Trainees can access their own profile, sessions, participant records, feedback, training history, and certification progress. Their assigned Instructor and authorized Administrators can access the information required to supervise and manage their journey.

After completing all requirements and receiving approval, a Trainee can be granted the Facilitator role.

## Facilitator

A **Facilitator** is certified and authorized to conduct Janzu sessions with Session Participants.

### Responsibilities

- Provide Janzu sessions professionally and responsibly.
- Manage their professional profile and practice locations.
- Manage availability, bookings, and session requests.
- Maintain appropriate records for their sessions.
- Receive and review Session Participant feedback.
- Keep certification and contact information current.
- Participate in the professional Janzu community.

### Access

Facilitators can manage their own professional activity and may appear on the public Facilitator Map when their profile is verified and they have consented to the relevant visibility settings.

Facilitator status does not automatically grant permission to supervise Trainees, approve certification, or administer the platform.

## Role Relationships

A person can hold more than one role when appropriate.

For example:

- A Facilitator may also become an Instructor.
- An Instructor may also facilitate professional sessions.
- An Administrator may also be an Instructor or Facilitator.

The key distinction is:

> A Facilitator practices Janzu with Session Participants. An Instructor teaches Janzu and guides Trainees.

Additional responsibilities should be granted through separate roles rather than assumed from a person's certification status.
