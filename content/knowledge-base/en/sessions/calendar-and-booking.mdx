Calendar availability lets you offer specific future times from your public practitioner profile. It does not expose your private calendar or session records.

## Choose Quick Add or Calendar

Open **Sessions**, then **Calendar availability**.

- Use **Quick Add** when you already know the exact date and time. Choose the start, duration, repeat pattern, and number of occurrences, then select **Add slot**.
- Use **Calendar** when you want to plan visually. Switch between **Month**, **Week**, and **Day**. Select a day or time to open its details, add a slot, or review that day's slots.
- **Month** gives an overview. **Week** shows hourly columns and may scroll horizontally on a phone. **Day** is the simplest view on a small screen.

Both methods create the same type of availability slot. Times must be in the future. A slot can last from 15 minutes to 8 hours.

## Create recurring slots

Choose **Every day**, **Every week**, **Every 2 weeks**, or **Every month**, then enter the total number of occurrences. A series can contain up to 52 slots. **Does not repeat** creates one slot.

Recurring slots are separate bookable times connected as one series. Cancelling the series cancels only its future occurrences that are still available. It does not cancel a booked slot.

## Understand booking states

- **Available** means the future slot appears on your public profile and a visitor can request it.
- **Booked** means a visitor submitted the request form for that slot. The slot is removed from public availability immediately so another visitor cannot request it.
- The new request appears under **Session requests** as **Pending**. Review it and choose **Accept** or **Decline**.
- **Cancelled** means you withdrew an available slot. It no longer appears to visitors.

The booking-slot state and request-review state describe different parts of the workflow. Accepting or declining a request records your review decision; it does not turn the slot back into public availability.

## Cancel a slot or series

You can cancel a slot only while it is **Available**. Select **Cancel slot** for one occurrence, or **Cancel series** for all future available occurrences in the same series.

Booked slots do not show a cancellation action. If a booking needs to change, contact the visitor using the private request details and review the request. The portal does not currently send an automatic cancellation message or restore a declined booking to availability.

## What visitors can see

Visitors see only future **Available** slots on a public practitioner profile. They do not see cancelled or booked slots, recurrence settings, private session history, private participant records, or the rest of your calendar.

Visitor contact details and messages are private. They are available only through authenticated portal workflows; the public profile never exposes them.

## Time zones

The portal stores each slot as an exact point in time and displays it in the time zone of the device viewing the page. Create availability using the local date and time shown on your device.

Before saving, confirm your device time zone, especially when travelling or scheduling across daylight-saving changes. A visitor in another time zone may see the same slot at a different local clock time.

## Desktop and mobile tips

On desktop, keep the guide beside the calendar while using the wider Month or Week layouts. On mobile, the guide stacks with the calendar. Use Day view for a compact schedule; Week view scrolls horizontally. Selecting a date or hour opens a full-height details panel where you can add or cancel availability.
