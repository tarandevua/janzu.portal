The Janzu Portal brings your community work, practice records, and shared resources into one secure place.

## Find your way around

Use the main sidebar to open your dashboard, session participants, sessions, locations, events, and community resources. The options you see depend on your portal role.

The **Knowledge Base** contains practical guides for common portal workflows. Use the article list on the left and the page outline on the right to move between topics.

## Your welcome email

After you first activate your account, the portal sends one welcome email in your saved English or Spanish preference. It summarizes your verified portal role, First Steps, the certification journey, profile privacy, and where to get help. Signing in again or changing roles does not send another welcome email.

The email never includes private session notes, Session Participant contact details, feedback text, authentication tokens, or training and assessment evidence. Its links require you to sign in and pass the same portal authorization checks as direct navigation.

## Get help

Start with [First Steps](/en/dashboard/knowledge-base/getting-started/first-steps). If a portal feature behaves differently from the guide, contact a portal administrator and include the page you were using.
