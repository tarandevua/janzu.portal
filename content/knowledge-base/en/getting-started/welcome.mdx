The Janzu Portal brings your community work, practice records, and shared resources into one secure place.

## Find your way around

Use the main sidebar to open your dashboard, clients, sessions, locations, events, and community resources. The options you see depend on your portal role.

The **Knowledge Base** contains practical guides for common portal workflows. Use the article list on the left and the page outline on the right to move between topics.

## Get help

Start with [Managing sessions](/en/dashboard/knowledge-base/sessions/managing-sessions) for the basic session workflow. If a portal feature behaves differently from the guide, contact a portal administrator and include the page you were using.
