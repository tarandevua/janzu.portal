Use the persistent **First Steps** checklist from your dashboard. Your progress is stored securely in your account and follows you across devices. The guide remains available after every item is complete.

## Learning Alliance

Version `2026-08-15-v1` is an operational, non-legal acknowledgement. By accepting it, you confirm that you intend to participate respectfully, protect private session and feedback information, communicate boundaries and availability clearly, use the portal records honestly, and seek support when a safety or relationship concern arises.

You may revoke the acknowledgement at any time. Revocation makes this checklist item incomplete but does not delete the audit history or automatically change certification. A future version must be accepted separately.

## Profile and visibility

Create your profile, then choose the audience for every eligible field. Fields start private. Trainees may choose private or authenticated-community visibility and never appear publicly. The public map contains only opted-in, verified Facilitators and Instructors; the separate community map may also contain opted-in Trainees and verified Practitioners. Both maps use approximate 0.1-degree city/region grid-cell centers. They never return stored exact coordinates or private location notes. Use the public and community previews below your profile visibility settings to check the currently saved result.

Exact coordinates, official names, login email, training evidence, and Instructor relationships remain private to authorized workflow participants.

WhatsApp has its own consent control. Enter an international number, choose private or authenticated-community visibility, and check the separate consent statement before saving. WhatsApp is never public. You can revoke consent at any time; revocation immediately removes the number from profile and map responses while retaining a protected audit record of the consent action and policy version.

## Training history

Record official Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3 training with cohort, dates, location, teaching Instructor, coursework status, and an evidence reference. A claim does not change your verified level until your active Instructor or an Administrator verifies it.

## Instructor selection

Choosing an Instructor sends a request; it does not assign the Instructor silently. The relationship becomes active only after acceptance. You may request a transfer, and current and historical relationships remain auditable.

## Calendar

Calendar times use the active locale and your device time zone. Review the displayed date, start time, duration, and availability before saving. Recurring availability creates a series of offered slots; a booking request remains pending until accepted or declined. Cancelling a booking does not erase its history. Public visitors see only explicitly published availability, never private calendar details.

## Sessions

Create a private Session Participant before logging a one-to-one practice session, or create the participant during the session workflow. Confirm the date, duration, and location before saving. Notes and participant contact information are private. Only completed sessions that meet the approved rules and receive feedback or authorized verification can count toward certification milestones.

## Feedback

Copy the unique feedback link from the relevant session and send it only to that participant. A submitted response validates the linked session. Feedback free text and participant contact data stay private; an assigned Instructor receives only the supervision summary permitted by policy. Use the exact feedback link in notifications to open the corresponding authorized record.
