The certification area shows your current step in the full certification journey. Steps unlock in order; reaching a session total does not skip training or an authorized review.

## What contributes to progress

A practice session counts after verified Level 1 when it is a completed one-to-one record of at least 60 minutes and has participant feedback or authorized validation. If a session is invalidated, the portal recalculates eligibility.

Verified Level 1 opens the practicum. At 25 counted sessions, an actively assigned Instructor is required before Level 2 review is available. Verified Level 2 then leads to the cumulative 50-session milestone. The count does not restart after Level 2.

## Review your progress

Open **Certification** from the community section of the sidebar. Review the highlighted current state and counted-session total. Locked later steps require their preceding training, practice, and authorized decisions.

## Approval

Meeting the displayed requirements does not automatically issue certification. Assessment, final Administrator approval, certificate issuance, and Facilitator activation use separate authorized workflows.

An Administrator may correct a workflow error only one step at a time and must record a reason and supporting evidence reference. The correction is retained in a private audit history.
