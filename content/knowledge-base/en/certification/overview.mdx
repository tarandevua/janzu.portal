The certification area shows your current step in the full certification journey. Steps unlock in order; reaching a session total does not skip training or an authorized review.

## What contributes to progress

A practice session counts after verified Level 1 when it is a completed one-to-one record of at least 60 minutes and has participant feedback or authorized validation. If a session is invalidated, the portal recalculates eligibility.

Verified Level 1 opens the practicum. At 25 counted sessions, an actively assigned Instructor is required before Level 2 review is available. Verified Level 2 then leads to the cumulative 50-session milestone. The count does not restart after Level 2.

## Request Level 2 review

When verified Level 1, 25 counted sessions, and an active Instructor are all present, **Request Level 2 review** appears in Certification. Your active Instructor receives the request and can approve readiness, reject it, or require revision. A readiness approval does not complete Level 2; the official Level 2 training still has to be completed and verified.

If a counted session is later invalidated or your active assignment ends, the portal withdraws stale eligibility. After the requirements are satisfied again, submit a new review request. The original milestone message is not sent again.

## Assessment after 50 sessions

After verified Level 2 and 50 cumulative counted sessions, request an assessment-readiness review. Your active Instructor checks the training, coursework, practice total, and current assignment. Approval opens the assessment queue but does not pass the assessment.

An Administrator assigns an authorized Assessor who is not your active Instructor. The Assessor records the date, outcome, private notes, and—when the assessment is incomplete, requires revision, or is not passed—an explicit next action. Complete that remediation with your Instructor. Once the Instructor verifies it, the portal opens a new assessment attempt while preserving the earlier result.

A passed assessment still requires the separate final certification and certificate workflow.

## Review your progress

Open **Certification** from the community section of the sidebar. Review the highlighted current state and counted-session total. Locked later steps require their preceding training, practice, and authorized decisions.

## Approval

Meeting the displayed requirements does not automatically issue certification. Assessment, final Administrator approval, certificate issuance, and Facilitator activation use separate authorized workflows.

An Administrator may correct a workflow error only one step at a time and must record a reason and supporting evidence reference. The correction is retained in a private audit history.

## Certificate and Facilitator access

After an authorized passed assessment, an Administrator reviews your official name and issues the final bilingual PDF. Issuance activates certification-derived Facilitator access at the same time. It does not make your profile or map location visible; your existing public and community visibility choices remain unchanged.

Download the active document from **Certification**. Required certificate email links back to this authenticated download and does not attach the PDF. The printed certificate number and QR code open a minimal public verification page. Your name appears there only if you already opted into a verified public professional profile.

If your official name later differs from the immutable certificate, request a replacement in Certification. An Administrator reviews the reason and issues a new number; the old document remains marked as replaced in history.

A revoked certificate cannot be downloaded as an active credential and public verification shows its revoked status. The private Certification record explains the decision and lets you submit an appeal. An approved appeal restores access by issuing a new certificate rather than reactivating the revoked document.
