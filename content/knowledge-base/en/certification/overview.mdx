The certification area summarizes progress recorded through eligible portal activities.

## What contributes to progress

Validated sessions and other requirements defined by the Janzu certification process appear in your certification view.

## Review your progress

Open **Certification** from the community section of the sidebar. Check that the displayed session totals match your records before requesting a review.

## Approval

Meeting the displayed requirements does not automatically issue certification. An authorized reviewer completes the final review and records the decision in the portal.
