Training history records official Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 training. A submitted claim does not change your current level until an authorized reviewer verifies it.

## Submit a record

Open **Training history** and provide the level, cohort, location, start and completion dates, teaching Instructor, coursework status, an evidence reference when available, and any private notes needed for review.

Your evidence reference and notes are private. Only you, your active assigned Instructor, and authorized Administrators can read them.

## Review and correction

Your active assigned Instructor or an authorized Administrator can verify the record or request a correction. A correction reason appears with a rejected claim. Use **Correct and resubmit** to update it.

Every submission, correction, and review preserves before-and-after audit snapshots. An Instructor loses access when the supervision relationship ends.

## Current level

The portal derives **Current verified level** only from verified records. A claimed or rejected training record cannot change it or unlock certification progress.

## Historical recognition

This form is not the two-person historical-member recognition process. Historical recognition requires the evidence, independent senior review, and Administrator decision defined by Janzu policy; do not use this form to bypass that process.
