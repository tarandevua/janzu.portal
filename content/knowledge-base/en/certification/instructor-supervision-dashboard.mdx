# Instructor supervision dashboard

Open **Instructor relationship** and find **Assigned Trainees**. Each active assignment shows the Trainee's verified level and training, counted-session progress, latest feedback rating, certification milestone, and a suggested next review.

Select any summary to open its authorized source record. Training links open the Trainee's training history, feedback links open the exact submitted feedback when one exists, and milestone links open the certification journey.

The dashboard does not show Session Participant identity, contact details, or written feedback. If an assignment ends or transfers, its supervision summary is removed immediately.

The next action is guidance based on records that already exist. Readiness requests and assessment decisions appear only when their dedicated workflows are available.
